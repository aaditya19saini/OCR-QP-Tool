Q.38 Let \(X_1, \ldots, X_n\) be a random sample from a \(N(0, 2\theta^2)\) population, \(\theta > 0\). Which of the following statements is (are) TRUE?
(A) \((X_1, \ldots, X_n)\) is sufficient and complete
(B) \((X_1, \ldots, X_n)\) is sufficient but NOT complete
(C) \(\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i^2\) is sufficient and complete
(D) \(\frac{1}{2n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i^2\) is the uniformly minimum variance unbiased estimator for \(\theta^2\)

Q.39 Let \(X_1, \ldots, X_n\) be a random sample from a population with the probability density function
\[ f(x \mid \theta) = \begin{cases} \theta e^{-\theta x}, & x > 0, \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}, \; \theta > 0. \]
Which of the following is (are) 100(1-\(\alpha\))% confidence interval(s) for \(\theta\)?
(A) \(\left( \frac{\chi^2_{2n,1-\alpha/2}}{2\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i}, \frac{\chi^2_{2n,\alpha/2}}{2\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i} \right)\)
(B) \(\left( 0, \frac{\chi^2_{2n,\alpha}}{2\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i} \right)\)
(C) \(\left( \frac{\chi^2_{2n,1-\alpha/2}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i}, \frac{\chi^2_{2n,\alpha/2}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i} \right)\)
(D) \(\left( \frac{2\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i}{\chi^2_{2n,\alpha/2}}, \frac{2\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i}{\chi^2_{2n,1-\alpha/2}} \right)\)
