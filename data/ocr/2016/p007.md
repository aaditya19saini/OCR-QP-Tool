Q.20 Let X be a random variable whose probability mass functions \( f(x|H_0) \) (under the null hypothesis \( H_0 \)) and \( f(x|H_1) \) (under the alternative hypothesis \( H_1 \)) are given by

\(\frac{X = x}{f(x|H_0)} \quad \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 0.4 & 0.3 & 0.2 & 0.1 \\ 0.1 & 0.2 & 0.3 & 0.4 \end{vmatrix}\)

For testing the null hypothesis \( H_0 : X \sim f(x|H_0) \) against the alternative hypothesis \( H_1 : X \sim f(x|H_1) \), consider the test given by: Reject \( H_0 \) if \( X > \frac{3}{2} \).

If \(\alpha\) is size of the test and \(\beta\) is power of the test, then

(A) \(\alpha = 0.3\) and \(\beta = 0.3\)

(B) \(\alpha = 0.3\) and \(\beta = 0.7\)

(C) \(\alpha = 0.7\) and \(\beta = 0.3\)

(D) \(\alpha = 0.7\) and \(\beta = 0.7\)

(C) \(\frac{1}{1+e^{-1}}\)
