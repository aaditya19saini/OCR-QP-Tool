Q.18
Consider the matrix

A = \(\begin{bmatrix} X_{11} & X_{12} & X_{13} \\ X_{21} & X_{22} & X_{23} \\ X_{31} & X_{32} & X_{33} \end{bmatrix}\),

where \(X_{ij}\), \(1 \leq i, j \leq 3\), are independent and identically distributed random variables with the probability mass function

f(x) = \(\begin{cases} \frac{1}{3}, & x = -1, 0, 1, \\ 0, & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}\)

The probability that A is orthogonal equals

(A) \(\frac{2^4}{3^8}\)

(B) \(\frac{1}{3^5}\)

(C) \(\frac{2^5}{3^9}\)

(D) \(\frac{2^3}{3^7}\)
