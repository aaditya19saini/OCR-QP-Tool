Q.25 Let \(X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_{10}\) be a random sample of size 10 from an \(\text{Exp}(\beta)\) distribution, where \(\beta > 0\) is an unknown parameter. Let \(T = \frac{1}{10} \sum_{i=1}^{10} X_i\). Consider the following statements.

P: \((\log_e 2)T\) is a maximum likelihood estimator of median of \(X_{(1)}\).

Q: \(e^{-\frac{2}{T}}\) is a maximum likelihood estimator of \(P(X_1 < 2)\).

Then

(A) P is correct and Q is NOT correct

(B) P is NOT correct and Q is correct

(C) Both P and Q are correct

(D) Neither P nor Q is correct
