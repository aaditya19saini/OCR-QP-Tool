Q.11 Let \(f: \mathbb{N} \to \{1, 2, 3, ..., 100\}\) be an onto function. Consider the following statements.

P: If \(g(n) = \sup\{f(2n), f(2n+2), f(2n+4), ...\}\), \(n \geq 1\), then
\(\lim_{n \to \infty} g(n) = \limsup_{n \to \infty} f(n)\).

Q: If \(h(n) = \inf\{f(n), f(n+1), f(n+2), ...\}\), \(n \geq 1\), then
\(\lim_{n \to \infty} h(4n+1) = \liminf_{n \to \infty} f(n)\).

Then

(A) P is correct and Q is NOT correct

(B) P is NOT correct and Q is correct

(C) Both P and Q are correct

(D) Neither P nor Q is correct
