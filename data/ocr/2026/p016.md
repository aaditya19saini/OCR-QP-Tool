Brand A    27    28    30    32    33
Brand B    17    19    20    21    23

(A) \(CV_A < CV_B\) and \(s_A < s_B\)

(B) \(CV_A < CV_B\) and \(s_A > s_B\)

(C) \(CV_A > CV_B\) and \(s_A < s_B\)

(D) \(CV_A > CV_B\) and \(s_A > s_B\)
