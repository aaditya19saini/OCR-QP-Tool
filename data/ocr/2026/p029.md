Q.30 Let \(X_1, X_2, X_3\) be three independent random variables such that \(X_1 \sim N(0, \frac{1}{2})\), \(X_2 \sim N(0, 2)\), and \(X_3 \sim N(0, 4)\). Consider the following statements.

P: \(\frac{16X_1^2}{2X_2^2 + X_3^2}\) has \(F\) distribution with 1 and 2 degrees of freedom.

Q: \(\frac{2X_1 - X_2}{2X_1 + X_2}\) has standard Cauchy distribution.

Then

(A) \(P\) is correct and \(Q\) is NOT correct

(B) \(P\) is NOT correct and \(Q\) is correct

(C) Both \(P\) and \(Q\) are correct

(D) Neither \(P\) nor \(Q\) is correct
