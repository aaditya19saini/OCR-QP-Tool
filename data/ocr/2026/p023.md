Q.24
Let \(X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n\) be a random sample of size \(n\) (\(n \geq 1\)) from a distribution with the probability density function
\[f(x) = \frac{1}{2\beta^3} e^{3x - \frac{e^x}{\beta}}, \quad x \in \mathbb{R},\]
where \(\beta > 0\) is an unknown parameter. Which one of the following is a maximum likelihood estimator of \(\beta\)?

(A) \(\frac{3}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} e^{X_i}\)
(B) \(\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i\)
(C) \(\frac{1}{3n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} e^{X_i}\)
(D) \(\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i^3\)
