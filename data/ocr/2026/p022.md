Q.23 Let \(X_1, X_2, \ldots\) be a sequence of independent random variables with \(P(X_n = n) = \frac{1}{n^2} = 1 - P(X_n = 0)\), for \(n = 1, 2, \ldots\). Consider the following statements.

P: \(\{X_n\}\) converges in mean-square to 0.

Q: \(\{X_n\}\) converges in probability to 0.

Then

(A) \(P\) is correct and \(Q\) is NOT correct

(B) \(P\) is NOT correct and \(Q\) is correct

(C) Both \(P\) and \(Q\) are correct

(D) Neither \(P\) nor \(Q\) is correct
