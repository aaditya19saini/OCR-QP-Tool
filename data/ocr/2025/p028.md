Q.26
Let \((X_1, Y_1), (X_2, Y_2), \ldots, (X_n, Y_n)\) be a random sample from a \(N_2(0, 0, 1, 1, \rho)\) distribution, where \(\rho\) is an unknown parameter. Which of the following statements is NOT correct?

MCQ2
(A)
\(\left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i^2, \sum_{i=1}^{n} Y_i^2, \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i Y_i \right)\) is a sufficient statistic for \(\rho\)

(B)
\(\left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i^2, \sum_{i=1}^{n} Y_i^2, \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i Y_i \right)\) is not a minimal sufficient statistic for \(\rho\)

(C)
\(\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i^2\) is an ancillary statistic

(D)
\(\left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i^2, \sum_{i=1}^{n} Y_i^2 \right)\) is an ancillary statistic
