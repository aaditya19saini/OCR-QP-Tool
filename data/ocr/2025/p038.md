Q.36
Let \(X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_{20}\) be a random sample from a \(N(\mu, \sigma^2)\) population. Suppose
\[
P = \frac{1}{10} \sum_{i=1}^{10} X_i \text{ and } Q = \frac{1}{9} \sum_{i=1}^{10} (X_i - P)^2.
\]
Then which of the following statements is/are correct?

(C) \(\frac{(X_{12} - X_{20})^2}{Q} \sim 2 F_{1,9}\)
