Q.8 Let X be a continuous random variable with probability density function \(f(x)\).

\(H_0: f(x) = \begin{cases} 
1 & \text{if } 0 < x < 1, \\ 
0 & \text{otherwise}, 
\end{cases}\)

against the alternative hypothesis

\(H_1: f(x) = \begin{cases} 
2x & \text{if } 0 < x < 1, \\ 
0 & \text{otherwise}, 
\end{cases}\)

MCQ1

(A) \(\alpha(1-\alpha)\)

(B) \(\alpha(2-\alpha)\)

(C) \(1-\alpha\)

(D) \(\alpha\)
