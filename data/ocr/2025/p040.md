Q.38
Let \(X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n\), where \(n > 1\), be a random sample from a continuous distribution with probability density function

\[f(x; \theta) = \begin{cases} \theta x^{\theta-1} & \text{if } 0 < x < 1, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise}, \end{cases}\)

where \(\theta > 0\) is an unknown parameter. Then which of the following statistics is/are sufficient for \(\theta\)?

MSQ2
(A) \((X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n)\)
(B) \((X_{(1)}, X_{(2)}, \ldots, X_{(n)})\), where \(X_{(r)}\) is the \(r^{th}\) order statistic, \(r = 1, \ldots, n\)
(C) \(\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i\)
(D) \(\prod_{i=1}^{n} X_i\)
