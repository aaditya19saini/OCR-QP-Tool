Q.40 Let \(Y_1, Y_2, \ldots, Y_n\) be i.i.d. discrete random variables from a population with probability mass function

\(\mathbb{P}(Y = y; \theta) = \begin{cases} \theta(1 - \theta)^y & \text{if } y \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{0\}, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise}, \end{cases}\)

where \(0 < \theta \leq 1\) is an unknown parameter. Assume the convention \(0^0 = 1\). If \(\hat{\theta}\) is the method of moments estimator of \(\theta\), then which of the following statements is/are correct?

(A) \(\hat{\theta}\) is also the maximum likelihood estimator of \(\theta\)

(B) \(\hat{\theta}\) is an unbiased estimator of \(\theta\)

(C) \(\hat{\theta}\) is a consistent estimator of \(\theta\)

(D) \(\frac{1}{\hat{\theta}}\) is an unbiased estimator of \(\frac{1}{\theta}\)
