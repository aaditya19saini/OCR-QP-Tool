Q.7
Let \(\{Y_k\}_{k\geq1}\) be a sequence of i.i.d. Bin(1, p) random variables, where \(0 < p < 1\)
is an unknown parameter. Let \(\hat{p}_n\) be the maximum likelihood estimator of p
based on \(Y_1, Y_2, \ldots, Y_n\). It is claimed that:

(I) \(\frac{\hat{p}_n - p}{\sqrt{\frac{p(1 - p)}{n}}}\xrightarrow{d} N(0,1)\) as \(n \to \infty\)

(II) \(\frac{\hat{p}_n - p}{\sqrt{\hat{p}_n(1 - \hat{p}_n)}}\xrightarrow{d} N(0,1)\) as \(n \to \infty\)

Which of the following statements is correct?

MCQ1
(A) (I) is correct and (II) is incorrect
(B) (I) is incorrect and (II) is correct
(C) Both (I) and (II) are correct
(D) Both (I) and (II) are incorrect
