Q.14 Let V be a subspace of \(\mathbb{R}^{10}\). Suppose A is a \(10 \times 10\) matrix with real entries.
Let \(A^k(V) = \{A^k x : x \in V\}\) for \(k \geq 1\) and \(A(V) = A^1(V)\). Which one of the
following statements is NOT true?
