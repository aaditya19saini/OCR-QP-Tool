Q.27
Let \((Y_1, Y_2, Y_3) \in \{0,1, \ldots, n_1^3\}\) be a discrete random vector having joint probability mass function

\(\mathbb{P}(Y_1 = y_1, Y_2 = y_2, Y_3 = y_3) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{n!}{y_1! y_2! y_3!} p^{y_1}(2p)^{y_2}(1-3p)^{y_3} & \text{if } y_1 + y_2 + y_3 = n, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise}, \end{cases}\)

where \(0 \leq p \leq \dfrac{1}{3}\) is an unknown parameter. Assume the convention \(0^0 = 1\). The maximum likelihood estimator of \(p\) is denoted by \(\hat{p}\). Which of the following statements is correct?

MCQ2
(A) \(\mathbb{E}(\hat{p}) > p\)
(B) \(\hat{p}\) is an unbiased estimator of \(p\), but not the uniformly minimum variance unbiased estimator of \(p\)
(C) \(\hat{p}\) is the uniformly minimum variance unbiased estimator of \(p\)
(D) \(\mathbb{E}(\hat{p}) < p\)
