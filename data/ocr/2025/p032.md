Q.30 Let \(\{X_n\}_{n\geq1}\) be a sequence of random variables, where \(X_n \sim \text{Bin}(n, p_n)\) with \(p_n \in (0,1)\). Which of the following conditions implies that \(X_n \xrightarrow{d} 0\) as \(n \to \infty\)?

MCQ2
(A) \(\lim_{n \to \infty} p_n = 0\)
(B) \(\lim_{n \to \infty} \mathbb{P}(X_n = k) = 0\) for each \(k \in \mathbb{N}\)
(C) \(\lim_{n \to \infty} \mathbb{E}(X_n) = 0\)
(D) \(\sup_{n \geq 1} \text{Var}(X_n) < \infty\)
