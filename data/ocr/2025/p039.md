Q.37
Let \(X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n\), where \(n > 1\), be a random sample from a \(N(\theta, \theta)\) distribution, where \(\theta > 0\) is an unknown parameter. Suppose

\[ T_n = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i \quad \text{and} \quad S_n^2 = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (X_i - T_n)^2. \]

Then which of the following statements is/are correct?

MSQ2

(A) \(T_n S_n^2\) is a consistent estimator for \(\theta^2\)

(B) \(T_n^4 S_n^{-4}\) is a consistent estimator for \(\theta^2\)

(C) \(\left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i, \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i^2 \right)\) is a complete statistic

(D) \(\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i^2\) is a complete sufficient statistic for \(\theta\)
