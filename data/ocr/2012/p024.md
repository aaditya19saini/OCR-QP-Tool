Q.23 (a) Let \(X_1, \ldots, X_n\) be a random sample from \(\text{Exp}\left(\frac{1}{\theta}\right)\) population. Obtain the
Cramer–Rao lower bound for the variance of an unbiased estimator of \(\theta^2\). (9)
(b) Let \(X_1, \ldots, X_n\) (\(n > 4\)) be a random sample from a population with mean \(\mu\) and
variance \(\sigma^2\). Consider the following estimators of \(\mu\)
\(U = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i\), \(V = \frac{1}{8} X_1 + \frac{3}{4(n-2)}(X_2 + \cdots + X_{n-1}) + \frac{1}{8} X_n\).
(i) Examine whether the estimators U and V are unbiased.
(ii) Examine whether the estimators U and V are consistent.
(iii) Which of these two estimators is more efficient? Justify your answer. (12)
