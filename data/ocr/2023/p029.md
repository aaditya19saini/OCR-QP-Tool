(A) \(\sum_{i=1}^{10} x_i = 140\)

(B) \(\sum_{i=1}^{10} y_i = 110\)

(C) \(\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{10} (x_i - \bar{x}) y_i}{\sqrt{\left(\sum_{i=1}^{10} (x_i - \bar{x})^2\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^{10} (y_i - \bar{y})^2\right)}} = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\)

(D) \(\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{10} (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{10} (y_i - \bar{y})^2} = 2\)
