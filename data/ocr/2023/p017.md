\(E(X^n) = (-1)^n \frac{2}{5} + \frac{2^{n+1}}{5} + \frac{1}{5}, \quad n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots\).
Then, \(P\left(\left|X - \frac{1}{2}\right| > 1\right)\) equals
(A) \(\frac{1}{5}\)
(B) \(\frac{2}{5}\)
(C) \(\frac{3}{5}\)
(D) \(\frac{4}{5}\)
