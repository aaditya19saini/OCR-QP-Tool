Q.36 Let \(f: \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}\) be a function defined by

\[f(x,y) = \begin{cases} \frac{xy(x+y)}{x^2 + y^2}, & \text{if } (x,y) \neq (0,0), \\ 0, & \text{if } (x,y) = (0,0).\end{cases}\]

Then, which of the following statements is/are TRUE?

(A) f is continuous on \(\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}\)

(B) The partial derivative of f with respect to y exists at \((0,0)\), and is 0

(C) The partial derivative of f with respect to x is continuous on \(\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}\)

(D) f is NOT differentiable at \((0,0)\)

(C) \(E(e^{tz})\) does not exist for all \(t \in (-\infty, 0)\)

(D) \(Z^2 \sim F_{1,1}\)
