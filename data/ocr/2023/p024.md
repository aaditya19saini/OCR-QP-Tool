\(\bar{x} = \frac{1}{10} \sum_{i=1}^{10} x_i = 0\) and \(s = \sqrt{\frac{1}{9} \sum_{i=1}^{10} (x_i - \bar{x})^2} = 2\),

then based on the values of \(\bar{x}\) and s and using Student's t-distribution with 9 degrees of freedom, 90% confidence interval(s) for \(\theta\) is/are

(A) \((-0.8746, \infty)\)

(B) \((-0.8746, 0.8746)\)

(C) \((-1.1587, 1.1587)\)

(D) \((-\infty, 0.8746)\)
