Q.18 Let \(X_1\), \(X_2\), \(X_3\), \(X_4\) be a random sample of size 4 from an \(N(\theta,1)\) distribution, where \(\theta \in \mathbb{R}\) is an unknown parameter. Let \(\bar{X} = \frac{1}{4}\sum_{i=1}^{4} X_i\), \(g(\theta) = \theta^2 + 2\theta\) and \(L(\theta)\) be the Cramer-Rao lower bound on variance of unbiased estimators of \(g(\theta)\). Then, which one of the following statements is FALSE?

(A) \(L(\theta) = (1 + \theta)^2\)

(B) \(\bar{X} + e^{\bar{X}}\) is a sufficient statistic for \(\theta\)

(C) \((1 + \bar{X})^2\) is the uniformly minimum variance unbiased estimator of \(g(\theta)\)

(D) \(\text{Var}((1 + \bar{X})^2) \geq \frac{(1 + \theta)^2}{2}\)
