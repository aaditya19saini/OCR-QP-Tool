Q.1 Let \( M = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ -1 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \). If a non-zero vector \( \boldsymbol{X} = (x, y, z)^\mathrm{T} \in \mathbb{R}^3 \) satisfies
\( M^6 \boldsymbol{X} = \boldsymbol{X} \), then a subspace of \( \mathbb{R}^3 \) that contains the vector \( \boldsymbol{X} \) is
(A) \( \{ (x, y, z)^\mathrm{T} \in \mathbb{R}^3 : x = 0, y + z = 0 \} \)
(B) \( \{ (x, y, z)^\mathrm{T} \in \mathbb{R}^3 : y = 0, x + z = 0 \} \)
(C) \( \{ (x, y, z)^\mathrm{T} \in \mathbb{R}^3 : z = 0, x + y = 0 \} \)
(D) \( \{ (x, y, z)^\mathrm{T} \in \mathbb{R}^3 : x = 0, y - z = 0 \} \)
