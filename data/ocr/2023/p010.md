Q.15 Let $X_1, X_2, \ldots$ be a sequence of $i, j, d$. random variables such that
\[
P(X_1 = 0) = P(X_1 = 1) = P(X_1 = 2) = \frac{1}{3}.
\]
Let $S_n = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i$ and $T_n = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i^2$, $n = 1, 2, \ldots$. Suppose that
\(\alpha_1 = \lim_{n \to \infty} P\left(\left|S_n - \frac{1}{2}\right| < \frac{3}{4}\right)\),
\(\alpha_3 = \lim_{n \to \infty} P\left(\left|T_n - \frac{1}{3}\right| < \frac{3}{2}\right)\) and
\(\alpha_4 = \lim_{n \to \infty} P\left(\left|T_n - \frac{2}{3}\right| < \frac{1}{2}\right)\).
Then, the value of $\alpha_1 + 2\alpha_2 + 3\alpha_3 + 4\alpha_4$ equals
(A) 6
(B) 5
(C) 4
(D) 3
