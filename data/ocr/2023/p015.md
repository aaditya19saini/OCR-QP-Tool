Q.20

Let \(X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n\) be a random sample from a \(U(\theta + \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{3}}, \theta + \sqrt{3} \sigma)\) distribution, where \(\theta \in \mathbb{R}\) and \(\sigma > 0\) are unknown parameters. Let \(\bar{X} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i\) and \(S = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (X_i - \bar{X})^2}\). Let \(\hat{\theta}\) and \(\hat{\sigma}\) be the method of moment estimators of \(\theta\) and \(\sigma\), respectively. Then, which one of the following statements is FALSE?

(A) \(\hat{\sigma} + \sqrt{3} \hat{\theta} = \sqrt{3} \bar{X} - 3S\)

(B) \(2\sqrt{3} \hat{\sigma} + \hat{\theta} = \bar{X} - 4\sqrt{3} S\)

(C) \(\sqrt{3} \hat{\sigma} + \hat{\theta} = \bar{X} + \sqrt{3} S\)

(D) \(\hat{\sigma} - \sqrt{3} \hat{\theta} \neq 9S - \sqrt{3} \bar{X}\)
