Q.22 Let X be a continuous random variable with the probability density function
\[f(x) = \frac{e^x}{(1 + e^x)^2}, \quad -\infty < x < \infty.\]
Then \(E(X)\) and \(P(X > 1)\), respectively, are
(A) 1 and \((1 + e)^{-1}\)
(B) 0 and \(2(1 + e)^{-2}\)
(C) 2 and \((2 + 2e)^{-1}\)
