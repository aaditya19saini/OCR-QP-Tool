Q.10 Let \(X_1, X_2, ..., X_n\) be a random sample from a \(N(\theta, 1)\) distribution. Instead of observing \(X_1, X_2, ..., X_n\), we observe \(Y_1, Y_2, ..., Y_n\), where \(Y_i = e^{X_i}\), \(i = 1, 2, ..., n\). To test the hypothesis

\(H_0: \theta = 1\) against \(H_1: \theta \neq 1\)

based on the random sample \(Y_1, Y_2, ..., Y_n\), the rejection region of the likelihood ratio test is of the form, for some \(c_1 < c_2\),

(A) \(\sum_{i=1}^{n} Y_i \leq c_1\) or \(\sum_{i=1}^{n} Y_i \geq c_2\)

(B) \(c_1 \leq \sum_{i=1}^{n} Y_i \leq c_2\)

(C) \(c_1 \leq \sum_{i=1}^{n} \log_e Y_i \leq c_2\)

(D) \(\sum_{i=1}^{n} \log_e Y_i \leq c_1\) or \(\sum_{i=1}^{n} \log_e Y_i \geq c_2\)
