Q.18 Let X be a single observation from a population having an exponential distribution with mean \(1/\lambda\). Consider the problem of testing \(H_0: \lambda = 2\) against \(H_1: \lambda = 4\). For the test with rejection region \(X \geq 3\), let \(\alpha = P(\text{TypeIerror})\) and \(\beta = P(\text{TypeIIerror})\). Then

(A) \(\alpha = e^{-6}\) and \(\beta = 1 - e^{-12}\)

(B) \(\alpha = e^{-12}\) and \(\beta = 1 - e^{-6}\)

(C) \(\alpha = 1 - e^{-12}\) and \(\beta = e^{-6}\)

(D) \(\alpha = e^{-6}\) and \(\beta = e^{-12}\)

Q.19 Let Y be an exponential random variable with mean \(1/\theta\), where \(\theta > 0\). The conditional distribution of X given Y has Poisson distribution with mean Y. Then, the variance of X is

(A) \(\frac{1}{\theta^2}\)

(B) \(\frac{\theta + 1}{\theta}\)

(C) \(\frac{\theta^2 + 1}{\theta^2}\)

(D) \(\frac{\theta + 1}{\theta^2}\)

(B) between 0.1 and 0.2

(C) between 0.2 and 0.3

Q.21 Suppose \(X_1, \ldots, X_n\) are independent random variables and \(X_k \sim N\left(0, k \sigma^2\right), k = 1, \ldots, n\), where \(\sigma^2\) is unknown. The maximum likelihood estimator for \(\sigma^2\) is

(A) \(\frac{1}{n} \sum_{k=1}^{n} X_k^2\)

(B) \(\frac{1}{n} \sum_{k=1}^{n} (X_k - \overline{X}_n)^2\)

(C) \(\frac{1}{n} \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{X_k^2}{k}\)

(D) \(\frac{\sum_{k=1}^{n} X_k^2}{\sum_{k=1}^{n} k}\)

(A) \(\chi_{10}^2\)

(B) \(10 \chi_2^2\)

(C) \(\chi_{20}^2\)
