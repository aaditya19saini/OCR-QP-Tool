Q.11 Let \(X_1, \ldots, X_n\) be a random sample from a Poisson(\(\theta\)) population, where \(\theta > 0\) is unknown. The Cramer-Rao lower bound for the variance of any unbiased estimator of \(g(\theta) = \theta e^{-\theta}\) equals
