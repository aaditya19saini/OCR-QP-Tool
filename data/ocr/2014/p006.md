(A) \(\frac{9}{10}\) (B) \(\frac{1}{10}\) (C) \(\frac{81}{100}\) (D) \(\frac{19}{100}\)

\(p_Y(0)=\frac{p}{2}\), \(p_Y(1)=4p^2\), \(p_Y(2)=1-\frac{p}{2}-4p^2\), \(0<p<\frac{1}{4}\). Then, among statistics X and Y,

(A) both X and Y are complete

(B) X is complete but Y is NOT complete

(C) both X and Y are NOT complete

(D) X is NOT complete but Y is complete
