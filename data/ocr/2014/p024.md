Q.41 Let \(X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_5\) be a random sample from a population with probability density function
\[
f(x \mid \mu) = \begin{cases}
e^{\mu - x}, & \text{if } x \geq \mu, \\
0, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}; \mu > 0.
\]
Find the likelihood ratio test of size \(\alpha\) for testing \(H_0: \mu \leq 1\) against \(H_1: \mu > 1\).
