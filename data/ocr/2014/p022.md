Q.40 Let the conditional probability density function of $X$, given $Y = y$ ($y > 0$), be given by

\[ f(x \mid y) = \begin{cases} e^{y - x}, & x > y \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} \]

and let $Y$ have the probability density function

\[ g(y) = \lambda e^{-\lambda y}, \; y > 0, \; \lambda > 0, \; \lambda \neq 1. \]

Find the marginal density of $X$. Also find the correlation coefficient between $X$ and $Y$.
