Then the distribution of \(U = \frac{\sqrt{5}(\bar{X} + \bar{Y})}{S}\) is
(A) \(N(0, \frac{4}{5})\)
(B) \(\chi_9^2\)
(C) \(t_9\)
(D) \(t_{23}\)
