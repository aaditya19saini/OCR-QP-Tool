(C) \(\bar{X}_n\) and \(S_n^2\) are independently distributed for all \(n \geq 2\)

(D) \(\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i^2 \xrightarrow{P} 2\), as \(n \to \infty\)
