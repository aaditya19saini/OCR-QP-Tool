\(\bar{X}_e = \frac{1}{25} \sum_{i=1}^{25} X_{2i}\), \quad \(\bar{X}_o = \frac{1}{25} \sum_{i=1}^{25} X_{2i-1}\),

(C) \(\frac{49S_o^2}{\sigma^2}\) has \(\chi_{49}^2\) distribution

(D) \(\frac{S_o^2}{S_e^2}\) has \(F_{24,24}\) distribution
