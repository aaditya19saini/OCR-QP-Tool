Q.7 Let X be a random variable having the Poisson distribution with mean 1. Let \(g: \mathbb{N} \cup \{0\} \to \mathbb{R}\) be defined by

\(g(x) = \begin{cases} 1, & \text{if } x \in \{0,2\} \\ 0, & \text{if } x \notin \{0,2\} \end{cases}\)

Then \(E(g(X))\) is equal to

(A) \(e^{-1}\)

(B) \(2e^{-1}\)

(C) \(\frac{5}{2}e^{-1}\)

(D) \(\frac{3}{2}e^{-1}\)
