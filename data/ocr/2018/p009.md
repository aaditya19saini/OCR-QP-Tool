Q.22 Let $X$ be a Poisson random variable with mean $\frac{1}{2}$. Then $E((X+1)!)$ equals
(A) $2e^{-\frac{1}{2}}$ (B) $4e^{-\frac{1}{2}}$ (C) $4e^{-1}$ (D) $2e^{-1}$

Q.23 Let $X$ be a standard normal random variable. Then $P(X^3-2X^2-X+2>0)$ equals
(A) $2\Phi(1)-1$ (B) $1-\Phi(2)$
(C) $2\Phi(1)-\Phi(2)$ (D) $\Phi(2)-\Phi(1)$

Q.24 Let $X$ and $Y$ have the joint probability density function
$f(x,y)=\begin{cases}2,&0\leq x\leq y\leq1,\\0,& \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$
Let $a=E(Y|X=\frac{1}{2})$ and $b=Var(Y|X=\frac{1}{2})$. Then $(a,b)$ is
(A) $\left(\frac{3}{4},\frac{7}{12}\right)$ (B) $\left(\frac{1}{4},\frac{1}{48}\right)$
(C) $\left(\frac{1}{4},\frac{7}{12}\right)$ (D) $\left(\frac{3}{4},\frac{1}{48}\right)$

Q.25 Let $X$ and $Y$ have the joint probability mass function
$P(X=m,Y=n)=\begin{cases}\frac{m+n}{21}, & m=1,2,3;n=1,2,\\0, & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$
Then $P(X=2|Y=2)$ equals
(A) $\frac{1}{3}$ (B) $\frac{2}{3}$ (C) $\frac{1}{2}$ (D) $\frac{1}{4}$
