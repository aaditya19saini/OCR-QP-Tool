Q.17 Let X be a continuous random variable having the moment generating function

\[ M(t) = \frac{e^t - 1}{t}, \quad t \ne 0. \]

Let \(\alpha = P(48X^2 - 40X + 3 > 0)\) and \(\beta = P((\ln X)^2 + 2\ln X - 3 > 0)\).

Then, the value of \(\alpha - 2\ln\beta\) is equal to

(A) \(\frac{10}{3}\) (B) \(\frac{19}{3}\) (C) \(\frac{13}{3}\) (D) \(\frac{17}{3}\)

Q.18 Let \(X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n\) (\(n \ge 3\)) be a random sample from Poisson(\(\theta\)), where \(\theta \in (0, \infty)\) is unknown and let

\[ T = \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_{i}. \]

Then, the uniformly minimum variance unbiased estimator of \(e^{-2\theta\theta^3}\)

(A) is \(\frac{T}{n}\left(\frac{T}{n} - 1\right)\left(\frac{T}{n} - 2\right)\left(1 - \frac{2}{n}\right)^{T-3}\)

(B) is \(\frac{T(T-1)(T-2)(n-2)^{T-3}}{nT}\)

(C) does NOT exist

(D) is \(e^{-\frac{2T}{n}}\left(\frac{T}{n}\right)^3\)

Q.19 Let \(X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n\) (\(n \ge 2\)) be a random sample from \(U(\theta - 5, \theta + 5)\), where \(\theta \in (0, \infty)\) is unknown. Let \(T = \max\{X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n\}\) and \(U = \min\{X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n\}\). Then, which of the following statements is TRUE?
