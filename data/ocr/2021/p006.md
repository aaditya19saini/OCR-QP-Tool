Q. 11 – Q. 30 carry two marks each.

Q.11 Let \(f\colon \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}\) be a function defined by
\[f(x,y)=\begin{cases} \dfrac{y^3}{x^2+y^2}, & (x,y) \neq (0,0) \\ 0, & (x,y) = (0,0) \end{cases}\)

Let \(f_x(x,y)\) and \(f_y(x,y)\) denote the first order partial derivatives of \(f(x,y)\) with respect to \(x\) and \(y\), respectively, at the point \((x,y)\). Then, which of the following statements is FALSE?

(A) \(f_x(x,y)\) exists and is bounded at every \((x,y) \in \mathbb{R}^2\)
(B) \(f_y(x,y)\) exists and is bounded at every \((x,y) \in \mathbb{R}^2\)
(C) \(f_y(0,0)\) exists and \(f_y(x,y)\) is continuous at \((0,0)\)
(D) \(f\) is NOT differentiable at \((0,0)\)

Q.12 Let \(\{X_n\}_{n\geq1}\) be a sequence of independent and identically distributed \(N(0,1)\) random variables. Then,
\(\lim_{n\to\infty} P\left(\dfrac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i^4 - 3n}{\sqrt{32\,n}} \leq \sqrt{6}\right)\)
is equal to

(A) \(\dfrac{1}{2}\)
(B) \(\Phi(\sqrt{2})\)
(C) 0
(D) \(\Phi(1)\)

Q.13 Consider a sequence of independent Bernoulli trials with probability of success in each trial as \(\dfrac{1}{3}\).
The probability that three successes occur before four failures is equal to

(A) \(\dfrac{179}{243}\)
(B) \(\dfrac{179}{841}\)
(C) \(\dfrac{233}{729}\)
(D) \(\dfrac{179}{1215}\)
