\[M = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ a & b \end{bmatrix}\]

(A) \(a + b = 0\)    (B) \(b = \sqrt{1 - a^2}\)    (C) \(ab = -\frac{1}{2}\)    (D) \(M^2 = I_2\)
