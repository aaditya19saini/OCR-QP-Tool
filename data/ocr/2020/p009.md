Q.30 Let \(X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n\) be a random sample from a distribution with probability density function

\(f_\theta(x) = \begin{cases} \theta(1-x)^{\theta-1}, & 0 < x < 1 \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}, \quad \theta > 0\)

(A) \(-\sum_{i=1}^{n} \log_e(1 - X_i)^2 < \chi_{2n,1-\alpha}^2\) (B) \(-\sum_{i=1}^{n} \log_e(1 - X_i)^2 < \chi_{n,1-\alpha}^2\) (C) \(-\sum_{i=1}^{n} \log_e(1 - X_i)^2 < \chi_{2n,\alpha}^2\) (D) \(-\sum_{i=1}^{n} \log_e(1 - X_i)^2 < \chi_{n,\alpha}^2\)
