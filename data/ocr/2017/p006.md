For testing the null hypothesis \(H_0: \theta_1 = \theta_2\) against the alternative hypothesis \(H_1: \theta_1 > \theta_2\), suppose that a test \(\psi\) rejects \(H_0\) if and only if \(\sum_{i=1}^{4} X_i > \sum_{j=1}^{4} Y_j\). The power of the test \(\psi\) at \(\theta_1 = 1 + \sqrt{2}\), \(\theta_2 = 1\) and \(\sigma^2 = 4\) is

(A) 0.5987 (B) 0.7341 (C) 0.7612 (D) 0.8413
