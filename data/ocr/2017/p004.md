Q.10 Let \(X_1, X_2, X_3\) be a random sample from a distribution with the probability density function

\(f(x|\theta) = \begin{cases} \frac{1}{\theta} e^{-\frac{x}{\theta}}, & \text{if } x > 0 \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}\), \(\theta > 0\).

Which of the following estimators of \(\theta\) has the smallest variance for all \(\theta > 0\)?
