Q.9
Let \(X_1, X_2, ..., X_n\) (\(n \geq 2\)) be a random sample from \(\text{Exp}\left(\frac{1}{\theta}\right)\) distribution, where \(\theta > 0\) is unknown. If \(\overline{X} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i\), then which one of the following statements is NOT true?

(A) \(\overline{X}\) is the uniformly minimum variance unbiased estimator of \(\theta\)

(B) \(\overline{X}^2\) is the uniformly minimum variance unbiased estimator of \(\theta^2\)

(C) \(\frac{n}{n+1} \overline{X}^2\) is the uniformly minimum variance unbiased estimator of \(\theta^2\)

(D) \(\text{Var}\left(E(X_n \mid \overline{X})\right) \leq \text{Var}(X_n)\)
