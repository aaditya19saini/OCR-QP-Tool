Q.34
Let \(\Omega = \{1, 2, 3, \ldots\}\) be the sample space of a random experiment and suppose that all subsets of \(\Omega\) are events. Further, let \(P\) be a probability function such that \(P(\{i\}) > 0\) for all \(i \in \Omega\). Then which of the following statements is/are true?

(A) For every \(\epsilon > 0\), there exists an event \(A\) such that \(0 < P(A) < \epsilon\)

(B) There exists a sequence of disjoint events \(\{A_k\}_{k \geq 1}\) with \(P(A_k) \geq 10^{-6}\) for all \(k \geq 1\)

(C) There exists \(j \in \Omega\) such that \(P(\{j\}) \geq P(\{i\})\) for all \(i \in \Omega\)

(D) Let \(\{A_k\}_{k \geq 1}\) be a sequence of events such that \(\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} P(A_k) < \infty\). Then for each \(i \in \Omega\) there exists \(N \geq 1\) (which may depend on \(i\)) such that \(i \notin \bigcup_{k=N}^{\infty} A_k\)
