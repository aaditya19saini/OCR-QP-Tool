Q.30 Let \(X_1\), \(X_2\), \(X_3\), \(X_4\) be a random sample from a Poisson distribution with unknown mean \(\lambda > 0\). For testing the hypothesis \(H_0: \lambda = 1\) against \(H_1: \lambda = 1.5\), let \(\beta\) denote the power of the test that rejects \(H_0\) if and only if \(\sum_{i=1}^{4} X_i \geq 5\). Then which one of the following statements is true?

(A) \(\beta > 0.80\)

(B) \(0.75 < \beta \leq 0.80\)

(C) \(0.70 < \beta \leq 0.75\)

(D) \(0.65 < \beta \leq 0.70\)
