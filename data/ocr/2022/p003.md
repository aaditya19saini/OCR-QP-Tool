\[f(x) = \begin{cases} \lim\limits_{h \to 0} \dfrac{(x+h)\sin\left(\dfrac{1}{x}+h\right) - x\sin\dfrac{1}{x}}{h}, & x \neq 0 \\ 0, & x = 0.\end{cases}\)

(A) \(f\left(\frac{2}{\pi}\right) = 1\)

(B) \(f\left(\frac{1}{\pi}\right) = \frac{1}{\pi}\)

(C) \(f\left(-\frac{2}{\pi}\right) = -1\)

(D) \(f\) is not continuous at \(x = 0\)
