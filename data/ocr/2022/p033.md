(A) \(X + Y\) and X - Y are independent

(B) \(2X + Y\) and X - Y are independent if \(2\sigma_1^2 = \sigma_2^2\)

(C) \(X + Y\) and X - Y are identically distributed

(D) \(X + Y\) and \(2X - Y\) are independent if \(2\sigma_1^2 = \sigma_2^2\)
