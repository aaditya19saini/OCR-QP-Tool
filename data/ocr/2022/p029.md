Q.31 Let $\{a_n\}_{n \geq 1}$ be a sequence of real numbers such that $a_n = \frac{1}{3^n}$ for all $n \geq 1$. Then which of the following statements is/are true?

(A) $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^{n+1} a_n$ is a convergent series

(B) $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n} (a_1 + a_2 + \ldots + a_n)$ is a convergent series

(C) The radius of convergence of the power series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n x^n$ is $\frac{1}{3}$

(D) $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n \sin \frac{1}{a_n}$ is a convergent series
