Q.21 In a store, the daily demand for milk (in litres) is a random variable having \(\text{Exp}(\lambda)\) distribution, where \(\lambda > 0\). At the beginning of the day, the store purchases \(c (> 0)\) litres of milk at a fixed price \(b (> 0)\) per litre. The milk is then sold to the customers at a fixed price \(s (> b)\) per litre. At the end of the day, the unsold milk is discarded. Then the value of \(c\) that maximizes the expected net profit for the store equals

(A) \(-\frac{1}{\lambda} \ln \left( \frac{b}{s} \right)\)

(B) \(-\frac{1}{\lambda} \ln \left( \frac{b}{s+b} \right)\)

(C) \(-\frac{1}{\lambda} \ln \left( \frac{s-b}{s} \right)\)

(D) \(-\frac{1}{\lambda} \ln \left( \frac{s}{s+b} \right)\)
